# Action Verbs

Categorized list of strong verbs to open bullets. Pick the verb that
**accurately describes the user's role in the work** — a contributor who
"led" a project is a fabrication; a contributor who "built" a component is
not.

The categories below correspond to the kinds of impact resumes are scored
on. Rotate verbs within a section — repeating "Developed…" six times looks
lazy.

---

## Lead / drive / own

For senior bullets where the user genuinely had decision authority.

> Led · Drove · Owned · Spearheaded · Directed · Headed · Orchestrated ·
> Championed · Established · Founded · Initiated · Launched · Pioneered ·
> Chaired · Coordinated · Mobilized · Steered

**Use only if the user had the authority to say no.** Otherwise prefer
*build* or *deliver* verbs.

---

## Build / create / ship

For engineers and makers — the most-used category in tech resumes.

> Built · Developed · Engineered · Designed · Architected · Implemented ·
> Created · Constructed · Crafted · Authored · Programmed · Coded ·
> Prototyped · Shipped · Released · Deployed · Productionized · Rolled out ·
> Delivered · Launched

For platform / infra:

> Provisioned · Configured · Containerized · Orchestrated · Wired ·
> Automated · Instrumented · Hardened

---

## Improve / optimize

For work that took something existing and made it better.

> Improved · Optimized · Reduced · Decreased · Cut · Accelerated · Sped up ·
> Streamlined · Tuned · Refactored · Rewrote · Modernized · Consolidated ·
> Unified · Simplified · Standardized · Strengthened · Hardened · Migrated ·
> Decoupled · Eliminated

Pair these with a measurable delta whenever the user has one. If they
don't, prefer a *build* verb that's accurate, rather than padding with
"significantly".

---

## Measure / analyze

For data, research, and analytics work.

> Analyzed · Measured · Quantified · Evaluated · Assessed · Audited ·
> Benchmarked · Profiled · Investigated · Diagnosed · Examined ·
> Interpreted · Modeled · Forecasted · Predicted · Computed · Calculated ·
> Estimated · Mined · Surveyed

---

## Research / discover

For research engineers, scientists, and academic CVs.

> Researched · Investigated · Hypothesized · Tested · Validated · Replicated ·
> Discovered · Identified · Characterized · Demonstrated · Proved ·
> Disproved · Authored (paper) · Co-authored · Reviewed · Refereed ·
> Presented · Published

---

## Teach / mentor / communicate

For lead/IC bullets that involve growing other people or communicating
outward.

> Mentored · Coached · Trained · Onboarded · Taught · Tutored · Advised ·
> Counseled · Guided · Demonstrated · Presented · Briefed · Authored
> (docs/RFC) · Documented · Published · Evangelized

---

## Collaborate / coordinate

For cross-functional, partnership, or stakeholder bullets.

> Collaborated · Partnered · Coordinated · Liaised · Aligned · Negotiated ·
> Facilitated · Mediated · Reconciled · Convened · Unified

Avoid "Worked with…" — every verb in this list is more specific.

---

## Banned openers

These do not belong at the start of any bullet:

- Responsible for…
- Worked on…
- Helped with…
- Assisted in…
- Was involved in…
- Participated in…
- Was tasked with…
- Duties included…

If the source bullet starts this way, the rewrite replaces the opener with
the most accurate verb from the categories above. If no accurate stronger
verb fits, the work probably wasn't the user's — consider whether the
bullet belongs on the resume at all.

---

## Tense

- **Past role** → past tense for every bullet.
- **Current role** → present tense for ongoing work, past tense for
  completed projects within that role. Pick one and be consistent within
  the role.

Tense mismatch is a recruiter red flag (see
[recruiter-checklist.md](./recruiter-checklist.md)).
