# Research / Academic CV Keywords

Vocabulary for research engineer, research scientist, PhD-candidate, and
academic CVs. Used in workflow step 9 when the target is research-shaped.

Research CVs differ from industry resumes:

- They are **longer** (no one-page rule), and length penalties from
  [scoring-rubric.md](../scoring-rubric.md) do **not** apply.
- They include sections industry resumes do not: Publications, Talks,
  Posters, Teaching, Service, Grants, Awards, References.
- Citation formatting is part of the score — see
  [scoring-rubric.md](../scoring-rubric.md) "Research-specific".

**Usage:** add keywords **only when supported by the user's experience**.
See [rewrite-rules.md](../rewrite-rules.md) §7.

For research engineers who lean industrial (ML/AI applied research),
branch to [ai-ml.md](./ai-ml.md) and combine.
